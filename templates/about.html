About CampusConnect ERP

A Modern Student Activity Management System designed to simplify campus administration.

Who We Are

CampusConnect ERP is a modern web-based Student Activity Management System developed to simplify academic and administrative activities within educational institutions.

The system provides separate secure portals for Administrators, Teachers and Students, making communication faster, easier and more efficient.

From attendance management to assignments, notices, events and student records, CampusConnect ERP keeps everything organized in one centralized platform.

Our Mission

To simplify campus management through technology by reducing paperwork, improving communication and providing a secure digital platform for students, teachers and administrators.

Our Vision

To build a smart, secure and paperless campus where every academic activity can be managed efficiently through a modern ERP system.

Project Objectives

Our primary goals while developing CampusConnect ERP.

  • ✅ Digital Student Management
  • ✅ Online Attendance System
  • ✅ Assignment Upload & Download
  • ✅ Notice Management
  • ✅ Event Management
  • ✅ Secure Login System
  • ✅ Responsive User Interface
  • ✅ Centralized Database

Our Services

CampusConnect ERP provides all essential academic management services.

Attendance Management

Teachers can record attendance digitally while students can easily monitor their attendance percentage.

Assignment Management

Teachers upload assignments with files and students can download them anytime.

Notice Board

Share important announcements and updates instantly across the campus.

Event Management

Manage workshops, seminars, competitions and campus events with complete details.

Student Management

Maintain complete student records securely in one centralized database.

Teacher Management

Administrators can manage teacher information, accounts and responsibilities.
