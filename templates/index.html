About CampusConnect ERP

A complete Student Activity Management System developed to simplify academic and administrative tasks.

Who We Are

CampusConnect ERP is a web-based application that helps educational institutions manage students, teachers, attendance, assignments, notices and campus events from one centralized platform.

The system provides separate dashboards for Administrators, Teachers and Students, making communication faster, easier and more secure.
